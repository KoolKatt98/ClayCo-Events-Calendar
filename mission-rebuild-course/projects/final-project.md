## PART 8: PERSONAL MISSION STATEMENT

*Module Reference: Module 11 — Life Design; Module 10 — Leadership and Legacy*

This is the last thing you write in this course. It should feel earned.

A Personal Mission Statement is not a slogan. It's not "be the best version of myself" or "serve others." Those are bumper stickers.

A real mission statement is:
- **Specific** to who you are
- **Honest** about what you care about
- **Grounded** in your values and your experience
- **Directional** — it tells you what to do when you're unsure
- **Yours** — it doesn't sound like anyone else's

---

### Requirements

- 3 to 5 sentences
- Written in first person
- Must reference: what you are building, who you are serving or leading, and what you stand for
- Must pass the "2 AM test": if you read this at your lowest moment, does it still hold? Does it still feel true?
- Cannot include: corporate-speak, generic virtue words used without definition, anything that could have been written by anyone

---

### Pre-Writing Prompts

Before drafting, answer these (1-3 sentences each):

1. What would the people who matter most to me say my life is about — right now?
2. What do I want them to be able to say in five years?
3. What am I most afraid I'll fail at — and why does that fear mean it matters?
4. What would I regret not building, not doing, or not becoming?
5. What does my service actually mean to me now, at this distance from it?
