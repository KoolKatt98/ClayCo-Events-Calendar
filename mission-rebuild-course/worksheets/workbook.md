---

> **How to use this workbook:** Print it or work in it digitally. Don't skip modules. The exercises build on each other. Be honest — nobody's grading you, and the only person you're bullshitting if you lie on these pages is yourself. Come back to this workbook in 6 months and read what you wrote. That's the real value.

### 8.2 — Disability Rating Calculator Framework

Understanding how the VA calculates combined ratings (it's not math you'd expect).

**How VA "whole person" math works:**
The VA does NOT simply add ratings. It calculates the "remaining" healthy person after each condition.

Example: 50% + 30% is NOT 80%.
- Start with 100% healthy person
- 50% rating takes 50% of that → 50% remaining
- 30% rating takes 30% of 50% remaining → 15% more taken
- Combined = 65% (rounded to 60%)
