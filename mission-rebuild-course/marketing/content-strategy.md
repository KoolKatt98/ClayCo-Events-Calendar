## POSTING NOTES AND PLATFORM GUIDANCE

### TikTok / Instagram Reels
- **Optimal length:** 60–90 seconds for educational content; 15–30 seconds for punchy list/text overlay content
- **Hook delivery:** First 3 seconds must be spoken or displayed — no silent intros, no music-only intros
- **Caption strategy:** Repeat the hook in the caption, add relevant hashtags (#VeteranTransition #MilitaryToVeteran #VeteranBusiness #VABenefits #VeteranFinance)
- **Engagement:** Reply to every comment in the first 2 hours — the algorithm rewards engagement velocity
- **Repurposing:** Most TikTok and Reels content can be posted on both platforms with identical or slightly modified captions

### YouTube Shorts
- **Optimal length:** 50–59 seconds (leaves room for CTA without going over)
- **Thumbnail:** Auto-generated from video, but front-load the most visually compelling frame
- **Description:** Include the full CTA, email sign-up link, and course link in every description
- **Repurposing:** Shorts can often be repurposed from TikTok/Reels content with a slight format adjustment for vertical framing

### LinkedIn
- **Long-form posts:** 150–300 words with line breaks every 1–2 sentences for mobile readability
- **Carousels:** 5–10 slides; first slide is the hook/cover, last slide is always the CTA
- **Tone:** Slightly more professional than TikTok but still personal and direct — LinkedIn rewards authenticity from individuals, not press releases
- **Hashtags:** 3–5 maximum. Use #Veterans #VeteranLeadership #MilitaryTransition #CareerDevelopment
- **Posting time:** Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am or 12–1pm local time for maximum reach
- **Engagement:** Comments generate reach — respond to every comment within the day

### Cross-Platform Content Repurposing Matrix

| Original Format | Repurpose to |
|----------------|-------------|
| Talking head video (TikTok) | Reels, YouTube Short, LinkedIn video |
| LinkedIn long-form text post | TikTok text overlay script |

### Lead Magnet Integration
- Every piece of content should have a path to the **Veteran Rebuild Checklist** (free lead magnet)
- Bio links: Use a link-in-bio tool (Linktree or similar) with the lead magnet as the primary CTA
- Comment automations: Set up keyword triggers ("AUDIT," "BUDGET," "VA LOAN," "APPEAL") to auto-deliver the lead magnet link or relevant resource via DM

### Content Batching Recommendation
- Film 10–15 talking head segments per session
- Design 4–6 carousel decks per session
- Write 8–10 LinkedIn posts per session
- Batch, schedule 2 weeks ahead, and monitor performance weekly
- Double down on any piece that exceeds average reach/engagement — repurpose it in a new format
