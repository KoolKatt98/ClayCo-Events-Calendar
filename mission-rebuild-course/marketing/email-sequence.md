This is where the practical work gets real. We do a complete financial audit — income, expenses, debt, net worth, trajectory. You will know your numbers by the end of this phase in a way you probably have never known them before. And then we go through every VA benefit you may be entitled to: disability compensation, VR&E education benefits, VA healthcare enrollment, VA home loan, VGLI, and more. The VA Benefits Maximizer Toolkit walks you through every program with templates and instructions. Most veterans in the program discover benefits they have been leaving unclaimed for years.

Your military career is not a liability. It is an unfair advantage — if you know how to present it. We cover the full career system: how to convert military experience into civilian language that recruiters respond to, how to optimize your LinkedIn profile so it works for you while you sleep, how to target the right roles, how to network without feeling like you're asking for charity, and how to walk into an interview and communicate your value without diluting it. Week 6 covers career acceleration: once you're in, how do you advance? How do you navigate corporate environments without losing your mind?

**Week 7: Real Estate and the VA Loan**
The VA home loan benefit is one of the most powerful financial tools available to any American — and most veterans never use it more than once. This module covers the fundamentals of real estate investing, the mechanics of house hacking (using a multi-unit property to generate income while you live in it), how to evaluate a deal, and how to use your VA loan as the foundation of a long-term wealth strategy. No hype. No gurus. Fundamentals, taught by someone who has done it.

**Week 8: Financial Systems and Wealth Building**
We move from knowing your numbers to building with them. Investment accounts. Tax strategy. Debt elimination sequences. Emergency fund architecture. The Veteran Wealth Runway Calculator. How to think about building generational wealth on a veteran's income and timeline.

**Week 9: Mental Resilience**
This is not therapy. If you need therapy — and many of us do — the VA and civilian options are available and I encourage you to use them. This module is about the practical, daily disciplines of mental toughness in civilian life: sleep, movement, community, purpose, managing stress in an environment that doesn't have the same release valves the military did. There is a section on alcohol and substances — honest, non-judgmental, and practical. Because it comes up, and pretending it doesn't is a disservice.

**Weeks 10–11: Relationships, Communication, and Civic Leadership**
The people around you are either assets or liabilities — and the way you communicate determines which category you fall into. Military communication is direct and efficient. Civilian communication is layered and contextual. We translate your communication style and help you rebuild relationships that took damage during your service. Week 11 moves into civic leadership: how to take everything you've rebuilt and use it to lead in your community, mentor the veterans coming behind you, and build a legacy that means something.

**Week 12: Mission Planning**
The program closes with a full mission planning session: what does the next 12 months look like, what are your metrics, who are you accountable to, and how do you carry the momentum of the rebuild forward without the structure of the program holding you up?
