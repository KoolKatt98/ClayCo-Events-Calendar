# MISSION REBUILD™ — Grading Rubrics

> These rubrics exist so you know exactly what an excellent submission looks like **before** you turn it in. No surprises. No guessing. If you hit all the criteria, you get full marks.

---

## RUBRIC 1: Weekly Module Assignments

**Applies to:** All module assignments (Modules 1–11)
**Total Points:** 100 per assignment
**Submission format:** Written document (PDF, Word, or Google Doc)

---

### Evaluation Categories

| Category | Weight | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Completeness | 25% | 25 |
| Depth & Specificity | 30% | 30 |
| Honest Self-Reflection | 25% | 25 |
| Actionability | 20% | 20 |
| **TOTAL** | **100%** | **100** |

---

### Category Definitions & Performance Levels

---

#### 1. Completeness (25 pts)

All required sections are addressed per the assignment prompt.

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds Expectations** | 23–25 | All sections complete. Goes beyond minimum requirements in at least 2 areas. Clear evidence of time invested. |
| **Meets Expectations** | 18–22 | All required sections addressed. Meets minimum word counts or deliverable specs. |
| **Needs Work** | 10–17 | One or more sections missing or superficially completed (1–2 sentences where 2–3 paragraphs were required). |
| **Incomplete** | 0–9 | More than half the assignment missing or not submitted. |

---

#### 2. Depth & Specificity (30 pts)

Answers are concrete, specific, and grounded in the student's real situation — not generic or abstract.

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds Expectations** | 27–30 | Every answer references specific people, numbers, dates, situations, or named experiences. No generic statements ("I need to work on discipline"). Shows clear thinking. |
| **Meets Expectations** | 21–26 | Most answers are specific. Some generic statements present but offset by concrete examples. |
| **Needs Work** | 12–20 | Predominantly generic. Reads like it could apply to any veteran rather than this specific student. |
| **Incomplete** | 0–11 | Vague throughout. Little evidence of actual self-examination. |

---

#### 3. Honest Self-Reflection (25 pts)

The student engages authentically with difficult questions rather than giving "correct-sounding" answers.

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds Expectations** | 23–25 | Demonstrates real vulnerability and honesty. Names struggles, failures, or contradictions without being asked. Evidence that the student challenged their own assumptions. |
| **Meets Expectations** | 18–22 | Generally honest. May avoid the hardest question(s) but engages meaningfully with most. |
| **Needs Work** | 10–17 | Feels performative. Answers what the student thinks the instructor wants to hear rather than what's true. |
| **Incomplete** | 0–9 | Minimal engagement with the reflection component. Surface-level or absent. |

---

#### 4. Actionability (20 pts)

The work product contains concrete next steps the student can actually execute — not vague intentions.

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds Expectations** | 18–20 | Next steps are specific, time-bound, and realistic. Student has identified barriers and addressed them. |
| **Meets Expectations** | 14–17 | Most action items are specific. At least one has a clear deadline or accountability mechanism. |
| **Needs Work** | 8–13 | Action items are vague ("I will work on my budget"). No timelines or accountability. |
| **Incomplete** | 0–7 | No actionable outcomes produced. Assignment remains purely theoretical. |

---

### Module Assignment Grading Scale

| Score | Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 90–100 | A | Outstanding — ready to move forward |
| 80–89 | B | Solid work — minor gaps to address |
| 70–79 | C | Acceptable — revisit weak areas before next module |
| 60–69 | D | Needs revision — resubmit recommended |
| Below 60 | F | Does not meet minimum standards — resubmission required |

---

### Resubmission Policy

Students scoring below 70 on any assignment may resubmit **once** within 7 days of receiving feedback. Maximum resubmission score: 80 points.

## RUBRIC 2: Mid-Course Project — The Civilian Rebuild Blueprint

**Assigned after:** Module 5
**Total Points:** 250
**Estimated completion time:** 4–6 hours
**Submission format:** Single document with clearly labeled sections

---

### Project Sections & Point Values

| Section | Points |
|---|---|
| Identity Map | 50 |
| Daily Structure | 45 |
| Financial Snapshot | 55 |
| Career Transition Plan | 55 |
| Resilience System | 45 |
| **TOTAL** | **250** |

---

### Section 1: Identity Map (50 pts)

**Required elements:** Pre-service identity, military identity, chosen civilian identity, 5+ defined core values, civilian identity statement.

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds (45–50)** | 45–50 | All five elements complete. Identity statement is specific, honest, and clearly the student's own — not borrowed language from the course. Values are defined with concrete behavioral examples, not just named. Pre-service and military identity sections show real reflection on what was gained and lost. |
| **Meets (35–44)** | 35–44 | All elements present. Identity statement exists and is personal. Values listed with basic definitions. Some depth in the reflection sections. |
| **Needs Work (20–34)** | 20–34 | One or more elements missing or perfunctory. Identity statement sounds generic or template-copied. Values listed without definitions or behavioral grounding. |
| **Incomplete (0–19)** | 0–19 | Multiple sections missing. Little to no genuine identity work evident. |

---

### Section 2: Daily Structure (45 pts)

**Required elements:** Personal operating system, morning/work/evening routine, non-negotiables, accountability system.

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds (41–45)** | 41–45 | Detailed, realistic schedule that fits the student's actual life (not an aspirational fantasy). Non-negotiables are specific and stated as commitments. Accountability system names a person or mechanism. Evidence the student has actually tested or thought through logistics. |
| **Meets (32–40)** | 32–40 | Full schedule present. Most elements addressed. Some aspirational elements present but grounded in reality. |
| **Needs Work (18–31)** | 18–31 | Schedule is vague or overly aspirational. Non-negotiables listed but not committed to. No accountability system. |
| **Incomplete (0–17)** | 0–17 | Skeleton outline only. No evidence of real planning. |

---

### Section 3: Financial Snapshot (55 pts)

**Required elements:** Income sources, monthly expenses (categorized), current debt, net worth estimate, VA benefits inventory, 6-month financial goal.

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds (50–55)** | 50–55 | All six elements complete with actual numbers (not "approximately" or "around"). VA benefits section goes beyond what's currently received to include what the student may be eligible for. 6-month goal is specific, measurable, and includes an action plan. Clear evidence the student did the math. |
| **Meets (40–49)** | 40–49 | All elements present with numbers. VA section addresses current benefits. Goal is specific if not fully planned. |
| **Needs Work (22–39)** | 22–39 | Numbers vague or estimated without effort. One or more required elements missing. VA section minimal. |
| **Incomplete (0–21)** | 0–21 | No real financial accounting done. Generic statements replacing actual numbers. |

---

### Section 4: Career Transition Plan (55 pts)

**Required elements:** Target industry/role (3 specific job titles), skills inventory with military-to-civilian translation, resume status, 5 networking targets, 90-day action plan.

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds (50–55)** | 50–55 | Three specific job titles with reasoning. Skills inventory uses civilian language with quantified evidence. Resume has a clear next step attached. Five networking targets are real people or organizations (not "LinkedIn connections in general"). 90-day plan has weekly milestones. |
| **Meets (40–49)** | 40–49 | All elements present. Job titles are specific. Skills translated with some evidence. Resume status noted. Networking targets named. 90-day plan exists with at least monthly milestones. |
| **Needs Work (22–39)** | 22–39 | Job titles vague ("something in tech"). Skills listed but not translated. Resume status unclear. Networking targets generic. 90-day plan is a list of intentions, not milestones. |
| **Incomplete (0–21)** | 0–21 | Multiple elements missing. No evidence of real career planning. |

---

### Section 5: Resilience System (45 pts)

**Required elements:** Current stress indicators, healthy and unhealthy coping patterns, personal resilience toolkit, support network map, one monthly commitment.

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds (41–45)** | 41–45 | Honest identification of both healthy and unhealthy patterns (no whitewashing). Resilience toolkit is specific and personal — not a copy-paste of the lesson examples. Support network map names real people and honest relationship quality. Monthly commitment is specific and made as a real commitment, not a suggestion. |
| **Meets (32–40)** | 32–40 | All elements present. Honest about at least one unhealthy pattern. Toolkit and network map have real content. Commitment is specific. |
| **Needs Work (18–31)** | 18–31 | Stress indicators vague. Unhealthy patterns minimized or absent. Toolkit is generic. Network map lacks specificity. |
| **Incomplete (0–17)** | 0–17 | Mostly skipped or token responses. No genuine resilience planning evident. |

---

### Mid-Course Project Grading Scale

| Score | Grade | Standing |
|---|---|---|
| 225–250 | A | Distinction track |
| 200–224 | B | Strong — on pace |
| 175–199 | C | Acceptable — address gaps before final project |
| 150–174 | D | Needs revision — resubmit before Module 9 |
| Below 150 | F | Required resubmission — schedule coaching call |

## RUBRIC 3: Final Project — The Next Mission Plan

**Assigned after:** Module 11
**Total Points:** 400
**Estimated completion time:** 6–10 hours
**Submission format:** Single comprehensive document with clearly labeled sections

This is the capstone. It should integrate everything from the course. Shortcuts here mean you're leaving value on the table — for yourself, not just for grading.

---

### Project Sections & Point Values

| Section | Points |
|---|---|
| 5-Year Civilian Roadmap | 60 |
| Financial Plan | 60 |
| Career Plan | 55 |
| Health Plan | 40 |
| Relationship & Support Plan | 40 |
| Benefits Strategy | 50 |
| Ownership & Wealth Strategy | 45 |
| Personal Mission Statement | 50 |
| **TOTAL** | **400** |

---

### Section 1: 5-Year Civilian Roadmap (60 pts)

**Required:** Year-by-year milestones across all major life domains (career, finance, health, relationships, purpose).

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds (55–60)** | 55–60 | All five domains mapped across all five years with specific, measurable milestones. Years build logically on each other. Evidence the student thought about sequencing (e.g., pay off debt in Year 1 before investing in Year 2). Milestones are ambitious but grounded in the student's current reality. |
| **Meets (44–54)** | 44–54 | All domains present. Most years have specific milestones. Some logical sequencing evident. Occasional vague goals but offset by specificity elsewhere. |
| **Needs Work (25–43)** | 25–43 | Some domains missing or years skipped. Milestones are wishes rather than plans. No evidence of sequencing or prioritization. |
| **Incomplete (0–24)** | 0–24 | Framework only. No real planning. |

---

### Section 2: Financial Plan (60 pts)

**Required:** Income goals, savings targets, investment strategy, VA benefits optimization, ownership goals.

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds (55–60)** | 55–60 | Income goals are specific and tied to the career plan. Savings targets have dollar amounts and timelines. Investment strategy names specific vehicles (TSP, Roth IRA, index funds, real estate). VA benefits section identifies what to apply for and when — not just what's already received. Ownership goal is defined with a timeline. |
| **Meets (44–54)** | 44–54 | All elements present. Most have specific numbers. VA benefits optimization addressed. Ownership goal named. |
| **Needs Work (25–43)** | 25–43 | Vague income goals. Savings targets without amounts. Investment strategy is generic ("invest more"). VA benefits not addressed beyond what's current. |
| **Incomplete (0–24)** | 0–24 | Financial plan is superficial or missing major components. |

---

### Section 3: Career Plan (55 pts)

**Required:** Role target, advancement path, education/certification plan, income trajectory.

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds (50–55)** | 50–55 | Specific role target with a 3–5 year advancement path named. Education/certs tied to that path (not random). Income trajectory shows realistic year-over-year growth with assumptions stated. Evidence the student researched their target field. |
| **Meets (40–49)** | 40–49 | Target role specific. Advancement path has 2+ steps named. At least one education or cert goal. Income trajectory present. |
| **Needs Work (22–39)** | 22–39 | Role target vague or undecided. No advancement path. Education goals random or absent. Income trajectory missing or "more money." |
| **Incomplete (0–21)** | 0–21 | Career section not meaningfully completed. |

---

### Section 4: Health Plan (40 pts)

**Required:** Training system, nutrition framework, sleep protocol, VA healthcare plan.

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds (37–40)** | 37–40 | Training system specifies modality, frequency, and volume. Nutrition framework is simple and sustainable — not a fad diet, not "eat better." Sleep protocol addresses the student's specific sleep issues. VA healthcare plan includes enrollment status and any pending claims or appointments. |
| **Meets (30–36)** | 30–36 | All four elements addressed. Training and nutrition are specific enough to actually follow. Sleep addressed. VA healthcare noted. |
| **Needs Work (17–29)** | 17–29 | Generic health goals. "Exercise more, eat better, sleep more." VA healthcare not addressed. |
| **Incomplete (0–16)** | 0–16 | Minimal health planning. |

---

### Section 5: Relationship & Support Plan (40 pts)

**Required:** Key relationships to invest in, community to build, mentors to find.

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds (37–40)** | 37–40 | Names specific relationships and what investment looks like in practice. Community goal is specific (type of community, how to enter it). Mentor search is active — names the type of person and one or two ways to find them. At least one relationship to repair or improve is named honestly. |
| **Meets (30–36)** | 30–36 | Key relationships identified. Community goal named. Mentor type described. Generally honest. |
| **Needs Work (17–29)** | 17–29 | Generic ("I want to build a community"). No specific relationships named. Mentor section vague. |
| **Incomplete (0–16)** | 0–16 | Relationship planning absent or perfunctory. |

---

### Section 6: Benefits Strategy (50 pts)

**Required:** Current benefits, what to apply for, education plan, home loan plan.

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds (46–50)** | 46–50 | Full benefits inventory completed. Identifies specific gaps and has a timeline for addressing them (e.g., "File supplemental claim for knee by [date]"). Education plan specifies school, program, and GI Bill chapter. Home loan plan addresses timeline and readiness. Shows clear understanding of benefits as a financial system, not just a safety net. |
| **Meets (37–45)** | 37–45 | Most benefits inventoried. At least one gap identified with a plan. Education and home loan addressed. |
| **Needs Work (21–36)** | 21–36 | Benefits list is incomplete. No gap analysis. Education or home loan plan absent. |
| **Incomplete (0–20)** | 0–20 | Benefits section not meaningfully addressed. |

---

### Section 7: Ownership & Wealth Strategy (45 pts)

**Required:** Real estate, business/side hustle, investments, cash flow targets.

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds (41–45)** | 41–45 | At least one ownership vehicle identified with a concrete action plan (e.g., house hack within 18 months using VA loan — here's the savings timeline and target market). Investment plan names accounts and contribution amounts. Cash flow target is specific with a timeline. Business or side hustle has a concept and first step identified. |
| **Meets (33–40)** | 33–40 | At least one ownership vehicle with a basic plan. Investment named. Cash flow goal present. Side hustle concept identified. |
| **Needs Work (19–32)** | 19–32 | Ownership goals vague. No specific investment vehicles named. Cash flow goal absent. Side hustle is an idea with no plan. |
| **Incomplete (0–18)** | 0–18 | Wealth strategy not meaningfully completed. |

---

### Section 8: Personal Mission Statement (50 pts)

**Required:** 3–5 sentences. Honest. Specific. Yours.

This is the hardest section to grade and the most important. A mission statement is not a LinkedIn bio. It should scare you a little — because it's real.

| Level | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds (46–50)** | 46–50 | Reads as uniquely this student's voice and life. Names what they're building, who it serves, why it matters to them, and how they intend to operate. Does not sound like a corporate values statement or a motivational poster. You can tell they wrote multiple drafts. |
| **Meets (37–45)** | 37–45 | Personal, specific, and honest. Covers purpose and direction. May sound slightly polished but genuine underneath. |
| **Needs Work (21–36)** | 21–36 | Generic. Could belong to any veteran in the course. Sounds borrowed from examples or course language. |
| **Incomplete (0–20)** | 0–20 | Absent, one sentence, or clearly not genuine. |

### Feedback Delivery

- **Module Assignments:** Written feedback within 5 business days of submission
